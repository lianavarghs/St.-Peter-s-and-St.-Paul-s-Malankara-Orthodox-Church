About Us

Welcome to St. Peter’s and St. Paul’s Malankara Orthodox Church, a vibrant faith community rooted in the ancient Orthodox Christian tradition. We are dedicated to worship, fellowship, service, and spiritual growth, guided by the teachings of Christ and the Apostolic Church.
